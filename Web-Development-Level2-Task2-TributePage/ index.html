The Biography

From a self-taught programmer in South Africa to the most influential entrepreneur of our time.

Elon Reeve Musk was born on June 28, 1971 in Pretoria, South Africa. As a child, he was an avid reader and taught himself computer programming at the age of 12. He sold the code for a video game called "Blastar" for approximately $500.

At age 17, he left South Africa to avoid mandatory military service and moved to Canada. He attended Queen's University and later transferred to the University of Pennsylvania, where he earned two bachelor's degrees in Physics and Economics.

In 1995, he moved to California to start a PhD at Stanford but dropped out after two days to pursue his entrepreneurial dreams during the dot-com boom.

His core philosophy has always been to solve existential problems for humanity. This led him to found companies focused on sustainable energy, space exploration, artificial intelligence, and neural interfaces — industries critical for the long-term survival and prosperity of civilization.

Key Milestones

1999
Co-founded X.com, an online financial services company. It later became PayPal and was acquired by eBay for $1.5 billion in 2002.
2002
Founded SpaceX with the goal of reducing space transportation costs to enable the colonization of Mars.
2004
Joined Tesla Motors as Chairman and lead investor. Later became CEO and Product Architect, leading the electric vehicle revolution.
2016
Founded Neuralink to develop brain-machine interfaces and The Boring Company to solve urban traffic congestion.
2022
Acquired Twitter for $44 billion and rebranded it to X, with a vision to create an "everything app".

Global Impact

SpaceX

First private company to send astronauts to the ISS. Pioneered reusable rockets with Falcon 9, cutting launch costs by 10x. Starship aims to make humanity multiplanetary.

Tesla

Accelerated the world's transition to sustainable energy. Made EVs desirable and mainstream. Also leads in solar energy and battery storage with Tesla Energy.

Neuralink & AI

Working on merging human brains with AI to solve neurological conditions. Also co-founded OpenAI to ensure AGI benefits all of humanity.
