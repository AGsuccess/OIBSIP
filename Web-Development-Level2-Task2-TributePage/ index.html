About Elon Musk

Elon Musk is a business magnate, investor, and engineer. He is the founder, CEO, and chief engineer of SpaceX; CEO and product architect of Tesla, Inc.; and owner of X, formerly Twitter. He is widely regarded as one of the most influential entrepreneurs of the 21st century.

Born in South Africa, Musk moved to the United States to pursue his goals in sustainable energy and space exploration. His vision is to reduce global warming through sustainable energy production and consumption, and to reduce the risk of human extinction by establishing human colonization on Mars.

Through Tesla, he revolutionized the electric vehicle industry. Through SpaceX, he made space travel more affordable and reusable. His work pushes the boundaries of technology, AI, and transportation.

Musk's approach combines bold risk-taking with first-principles thinking, inspiring millions of engineers and entrepreneurs worldwide to think bigger and solve humanity's biggest problems.

Key Achievements & Timeline

  • 1995: Co-founded Zip2, a web software company
  • 1999: Co-founded X.com, which became PayPal
  • 2002: Founded SpaceX to reduce space transportation costs
  • 2004: Joined Tesla Motors and led EV revolution
  • 2016: Founded Neuralink and The Boring Company
  • 2022: Acquired Twitter and renamed it X
  • 2024: SpaceX Starship makes major progress toward Mars missions
